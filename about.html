Our story

Care that is personal, practical and professional

Palufor Skin Clinic has supported Johannesburg clients with professional skincare and wellness services since 2014.

Our background

We began as a small beauty and wellness practice for clients needing trusted support for skin concerns and regular personal care. Our growth has come through referrals, repeat visits and consistent service.

Our team of three keeps every skin consultation focused and welcoming.

What guides our work

Our mission

To help people feel confident through high-quality, accessible skincare treatments.

Our vision

To grow into a recognised South African beauty brand without losing personal care.

Our values

Professional standards, honest guidance, client comfort, confidentiality and consistent care.

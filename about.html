Our Background

Palufor Skin Clinic was established in 2014 in Johannesburg as a small beauty and wellness practice focused on practical, affordable, and professional care. The clinic began by serving local clients who needed trusted support for skin concerns and personal care treatments. Over time, we grew through referrals and repeat customers who value consistent service and a welcoming environment.

Mission

Our mission is to help individuals feel confident and beautiful, both inside and out, by providing high-quality skincare and wellness treatments.

Vision

Our vision is to expand into a recognised beauty brand with a wider audience and multiple service locations across South Africa, while maintaining personal and professional care.

Who We Serve

We work with clients from different backgrounds, especially those looking for effective skincare and regular beauty maintenance. Our main audience includes:

  • Individuals experiencing acne or ongoing skin concerns
  • Clients interested in facials, waxing, and pedicure treatments
  • Customers seeking relaxation through massage therapies
  • Young adults and working professionals with busy lifestyles

Our Values and Service Philosophy

We believe beauty care should be accessible, respectful, and results-focused. Our approach combines proper treatment methods with clear communication so that each client understands their options. We aim to support both physical appearance and emotional well-being through every appointment.

  • Professional treatment standards
  • Affordable service options
  • Client comfort and confidentiality
  • Consistent care and practical recommendations

Our Team

Palufor Skin Clinic operates with a dedicated team of three employees. As a small team, we provide focused attention to each client and maintain a personal, supportive service experience.

Welcome to Palufor Skin Clinic. Since 2014, we have supported clients with skincare and personal care treatments that improve both appearance and emotional well-being.

Who We Are

Palufor Skin Clinic is a small South African beauty and wellness business based in Johannesburg. Our team of three focuses on quality treatment, professional care, and pricing that stays accessible for students, young adults, and working professionals.

Our Promise

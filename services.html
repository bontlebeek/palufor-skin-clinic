Our Treatments and Support Options

Palufor Skin Clinic offers skincare and personal care treatments for clients who want to improve skin health, maintain grooming routines, and enjoy relaxation therapy. Services are available by enquiry, and treatment combinations can be discussed based on your needs.

Beauty and Wellness Services

Facials

Facial treatments are designed to support skin cleansing, hydration, and improvement of common concerns such as acne and uneven texture.

Waxing

Waxing services focus on clean and professional hair removal with attention to skin comfort and hygiene.

Pedicures

Pedicure treatments help maintain healthy and neat feet, suitable for regular beauty maintenance.

Massage Therapies

Massage options are available for clients seeking stress relief and physical relaxation as part of overall wellness care.

Skin Consultations

Consultations provide guidance on treatment planning and product recommendations for individual skin goals.
